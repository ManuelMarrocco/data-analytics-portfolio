# CO2 Emissions Analysis

## Project Objective

The objective of this project was to analyze global CO2 emissions over a 25-year period, identifying:

- Countries with the highest cumulative emissions  
- Countries with the fastest emission growth  
- Countries with relatively stable emission levels  
- Countries showing emission reduction trends  

---

## Dataset

The dataset was sourced from Kaggle and contains:

- Country names and ISO-3 country codes  
- Annual CO2 emission values (measured in kilotons, kt)  
- Time series data across multiple years  

During the exploration phase, the dataset also included aggregated regions and non-country entities. ## Key Findings

The analysis highlighted a strong concentration of CO2 emissions among a small number of countries:

- The highest emission levels are concentrated in a few major economies, particularly the United States, China, and India  
- A significant gap exists between China and India in terms of total emissions over the analyzed period  
- China shows the strongest growth trend in emissions  
- The United States remains the largest overall emitter in absolute terms during the period considered

## Tools & Methods

- Python (Pandas)
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Data transformation (wide to long format)
- Time series analysis
- Trend evaluation using slope calculation
